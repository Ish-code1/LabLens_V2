# LabLens 3-Minute Demo Script

This script provides a smooth, end-to-end walkthrough of LabLens' core features, taking about 3 minutes to narrate and execute.

## 1. Introduction & Mode Overview (0:00 - 0:30)
*Launch `python main.py`. Keep your hand out of frame initially.*

**Narrator:** "Welcome to LabLens, a completely hardware-free, gesture-controlled chemistry lab. Using just a standard webcam, we can turn any desk into an interactive inorganic chemistry workbench."

*Bring hand into frame. Point to the UI zones.*

**Narrator:** "The interface consists of our main Reaction Lab. Notice the glowing cyan dot perfectly tracking my index finger—this is our primary interaction point."

## 2. Reagent Loading & Reacting (0:30 - 1:30)
*Hover over the top-left test tube icon. The Reagent Selector opens.*

**Narrator:** "To start, I just hover over the reagent icon. Let's explore the D-Block."
*Hover over the 'D' tab, then move your finger over the `FeCl3` pill to load it. A distinct amber glow immediately surrounds your finger.*

**Narrator:** "I've just loaded Iron(III) Chloride onto my finger. The glow corresponds to the real-world color of the salt."
*Move your hand down to the beaker and hold it there briefly. The notification confirms it was added.*

**Narrator:** "I've dropped it into our beaker. Now, let's add some Sodium Hydroxide."
*Open the reagent selector again, navigate to S-Block, and load `NaOH`. Move it to the beaker.*

## 3. The Reaction & AI Examiner (1:30 - 2:15)
*As `NaOH` is dropped into the beaker, the `Fe(OH)3` precipitate animation begins (a growing, reddish-brown blob).*

**Narrator:** "Watch the beaker. We get an immediate visual simulation—a reddish-brown gelatinous precipitate forming."
*Point to the Info Panel on the right.*

**Narrator:** "Crucially, the Info Panel instantly populates with the balanced equation, the specific observation, and an exam relevance tag telling students exactly how important this reaction is for the JEE."

*Press the 'a' key on your keyboard.*

**Narrator:** "And if a student gets stuck or wants to test their understanding, they can trigger the AI Examiner. LabLens passes exactly this reaction context—and nothing else—to Claude, forcing the AI to act as a strict Socratic tutor without hallucinating."
*Wait a few seconds for the AI Question to appear at the bottom of the Info Panel.*

## 4. Periodic Table Mode (2:15 - 2:45)
*Make a clear, deliberate FIST gesture. The screen immediately transitions to the Periodic Table grid.*

**Narrator:** "By simply making a fist, I can seamlessly transition into the Periodic Table mode. This acts as our secondary learning screen."
*Hover your finger over the `Fe` (Iron) cell.*

**Narrator:** "Hovering gives me quick stats, but if I hold my finger steady..."
*Keep your finger perfectly still over `Fe` for 1.5 seconds. The Character Card overlay appears.*

**Narrator:** "...I pull up a detailed character card showing its exact electronic configuration, period, and block."

## 5. Conclusion (2:45 - 3:00)
*Move your finger off the Character Card to dismiss it, then make another FIST gesture to return to the Reaction Lab.*

**Narrator:** "And with another fist, I'm right back in the lab. LabLens proves that with just a webcam and zero specialized hardware, we can deliver a premium, exam-indexed, interactive chemistry experience. Thank you."
